# Journaling, "memories" and photo-timeline apps: UI/UX references for the Life panel (day / month / year / on-this-day)

Scope note: researched 2026-11-06. Version labels are given inline. Most help pages fetched had screenshots without alt text, so screenshot contents could rarely be verified. Where that is the case it is listed under Gaps rather than guessed. Claims marked "(search snippet)" come from a search-result summary of a page that could not be fetched in full; treat them as lower confidence.

## 1. Day One: calendar, map, media, On This Day, timeline, and metadata on entries

### Takeaway
Day One has four peer views (Timeline/List, Photos/Media, Map, Calendar) plus a per-date "Today view". The calendar is the closest analogue to the Life month view. Each day cell is tinted with the journal's colour, or optionally shows one photo per day that the user picks ("Favorite"). Empty days stay neutral. Clicking a day opens the Today view, which combines that date's entries, suggested sources (Photos, Places, Events) and On This Day. On This Day reached the web app in September 2025, under a "Today" tab, ordered newest year first.

### Cited Findings
- **macOS view set (current help page):** the views are Timeline ("a chronological view of entries"; clicking one opens it in the right pane; Cmd-click opens several at once), Photos ("A view of all media", with filters above to narrow by media type), Map, and Calendar. Clicking a view's icon again scrolls back to the top. — [Day One: Journal views for macOS](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/journal-views-in-day-one-for-macos/)
- **Map:** entries are pins at their locations, and clicking a pin opens its entries. "Click and hold on a location in the map to start a new entry at a specific location." — [Day One: Journal views for macOS](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/journal-views-in-day-one-for-macos/)
- **iOS list:** entries from the same date are grouped under one date heading, and tapping that heading opens the Today view. Media filters cover Photos, Video, Audio and PDF. Tapping the active view's name again scrolls to the current date or most recent entry. — [Day One: Journal views for iOS](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/day-one-ios/journal-views-in-day-one-for-ios/)
- **Calendar cell colour:** "The days that have entries will be colored according to your selected journal." "Any days that do not have entries will be white or black, depending on your device appearance settings." — [Day One: Calendar view](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/calendar-view-in-day-one/)
- **Calendar cover photo:** "Photos from entries can now be enabled to show in the date cells" (Settings > Appearance > Show Photos in Calendar). The cover is chosen by the user. On iOS you long-press an image in an entry and pick Favorite; on Mac you right-click it and pick Favorite. "Android will use the first image added to an entry" and has no Favorite option. — [Day One: Calendar view](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/calendar-view-in-day-one/)
- **Calendar interaction:** on iOS and Android, "Tap any date to open the Today View" and long-press for a context menu. On macOS, clicking a date shows its entries and Today-view data, and right-click creates an entry on that date. On the web, clicking a date shows its entries and a "+" button adds one. Tapping the Calendar tab again scrolls back to the current date. iOS can jump to a date through search. The week start follows the locale, except on the web, where it is always Sunday. — [Day One: Calendar view](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/calendar-view-in-day-one/)
- **Today view as a day aggregator:** "The Today view shows entries created on that date." It also shows entry *sources* (Photos, Places, Events) that can be tapped to create an entry from that content. The "daily location summary" is iOS-only. The calendar also lets you "view On This Day entries." — [Day One: Calendar view](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/calendar-view-in-day-one/)
- **On This Day (marketing page):** a way to "revisit past entries, photos & videos, and locations from over the years" and a "visual feast of photos and videos you've captured". The page's illustration includes a map, but the text does not say how locations are drawn. — [Day One: On This Day](https://dayoneapp.com/features/on-this-day/)
- **On This Day on the web (2025-09-09):** a new "Today" tab with an "On This Day" button. Entries from past years appear in "reverse-chronological order", and the page calls the feature a "personal time capsule". The same release made new web entries include the current location and local weather automatically. — [Day One release: On This Day now available on web](https://dayoneapp.com/releases/on-this-day-now-available-on-web/)
- **Calendar and On This Day draw only from "All Entries":** a journal with "Show in All Entries" turned off looks empty on the calendar (search snippet). — [Day One: On This Day view](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/tips-and-tutorials/on-this-day-view/)
- **What users ask for:** forum requests to widen On This Day to "last week" and "last month", and to make it customizable (search snippets; threads not read in full). — [Day One forum: add last week / last month](https://forums.dayoneapp.com/forums/topic/add-last-week-and-last-month-to-on-this-day-feature); [Day One forum: customize On This Day](https://forums.dayoneapp.com/forums/topic/feature-request-customize-on-this-day/)
- **Comparable app (Journey):** the calendar is monthly and scrolls infinitely. "If a day contains at least one entry, the grid will display a color or a photo/video thumbnail." A calendar icon jumps across months and years, and an entry added to a past day defaults to 12:00 AM. — [Journey: Calendar interface](https://support.journey.cloud/en/categories/app-interface-functionalities/articles/calendar-interface-in-journey)

### Inferences
- The "user-pinned cover photo overrides the automatic pick" pattern (Day One Favorite) maps directly onto the month grid: auto-pick a cover from Immich, then let the user pin one per day and persist it.
- Day One's Today view is essentially Life's day page: entries plus suggested sources (photos, places, events) for one date, with On This Day reachable from it. A link from each day page to "this date in other years" follows an established pattern.
- Neutral empty cells (white or black by theme), with no "no data" icon, are the common convention. Day One and Journey both mark only days that have content.
- Clicking a day's map location to start a new entry is a cheap and useful affordance for a desktop map ("add note here").

### Gaps
- No fetched page describes how an entry card in the Timeline shows weather, temperature or location (icon, position, units). The Sept 2025 web release confirms location and weather are captured, but not how they are displayed.
- I could not check the size of the calendar thumbnails, whether a cell shows an entry count, or whether On This Day has per-year headers ("1 year ago" and so on). The screenshots had no alt text.
- I found no Day One year or heatmap view.

## 2. Apple Journal (iOS 17.2 → iOS 18 → iOS/iPadOS/macOS 26): suggestions, "moments", insights, map

### Takeaway
Apple Journal's distinctive idea is the **suggestion**, or "moment". On the device, it bundles one outing's heterogeneous data (photos, a place, a route map, music played, workout or steps, contacts) into a single card that can be saved as an entry with or without writing. Entry cards show a compact media grid on a soft coloured background. Browsing by date was missing at launch. iOS 18 added an Insights page with a dot-calendar and streaks, and the 26 releases (Sept 2025) added Mac and iPad, multiple journals, inline images and a map of entries. Reviewers say the Mac/iPad version feels like a stretched iPhone app, and the richest suggestion data stays iPhone-only.

### Cited Findings
- **Launch (Dec 2023, iOS 17.2, iPhone only):** suggestions are generated on the device, and the user picks which moments go into an entry (search snippet of Apple's press release). — [Apple Newsroom, Dec 2023](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/12/apple-launches-journal-app-a-new-app-for-reflecting-on-everyday-moments/)
- **How a suggestion is composed (MacStories, Dec 2023):** suggestions "intelligently try to group together your photos, workouts, places you visited, contacts you've interacted with, or even music you've listened to." The reviewer's example was a Lyon trip suggestion that combined photos, music from the train, a location pin and a high step count. After tapping a suggestion you choose which items to keep, or tap "Save Without Writing". The "+" button opens a full-screen dialog with suggestions, "reflection prompts in the form of open questions", and a blank entry. — [MacStories: Apple's Journal App](https://www.macstories.net/reviews/apples-journal-app-journaling-for-all/)
- **How entries look in the feed:** each entry is a card on a "softly colored background". Attached items are "arranged in a compact grid that's complimented with subtle gradients". Tapping an item opens it full-screen. At launch you could filter only by content type or bookmarks, with no date browsing. Criticisms: a cap of 13 photos, no search, and a worry that the feed won't scale over the years. — [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/reviews/apples-journal-app-journaling-for-all/)
- **A richer suggestion (iOS 26 preview):** listening to a new album while walking in a park produced a suggestion that bundled the playlist, the **route map** and the walk's photos (search snippet). — [BGR: How Apple plans to make Journal cool again in iOS 26](https://bgr.com/1922123/ios-26-journal-preview-ipad-mac-app/)
- **iOS 18 Insights (2024):** an overview page with current writing streak, total words, days journaled and places visited. A calendar marks days: "Days you journaled will be marked with a purple dot", and tapping a date jumps to it. Search filters are Photos, Videos, Music & Podcasts, Activity, Places and Text Only. State of Mind is logged on a scale from "very pleasant" to "very unpleasant". Streak widgets are available on the Home and Lock Screens. — [9to5Mac: Everything new in Journal for iOS 18](https://9to5mac.com/heres-everything-new-in-the-journal-app-for-ios-18/)
- **Streak tile:** the Current Streak tile can switch between daily and weekly streaks, and a weekly streak continues as long as you write at least once a week (search snippet). — [TapSmart: Insights in Journal](https://www.tapsmart.com/tips-and-tricks/insights-in-journal/)
- **26 releases (announced June 2025, shipped Sept 2025):** multiple journals ("You can now create multiple different journals to organize your entries"); images inline with text; drawings and handwriting; "a new map interface based on where you wrote them"; better search; recovery of deleted entries; "a collapsible sidebar where you'll find access to features like Insights, settings, and more". — [9to5Mac, 2025-06-17](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/17/apples-journal-app-is-coming-to-ipad-and-mac-with-big-upgrades/)
- **Per-journal identity:** each journal has its own name, icon and colour, and journals can be reordered (search snippet). — [iGeeksBlog: How to use Journal](https://www.igeeksblog.com/how-to-use-journal-app-on-iphone/)
- **The map needs location data:** without location on entries, or with permissions off, "there may be little to browse geographically" (search snippet). — [iGeeksBlog](https://www.igeeksblog.com/how-to-use-journal-app-on-iphone/)
- **Mac (macOS 26):** good for "fast writing and stream-of-consciousness note capture". Photo suggestions, workout data and other context prompts are iPhone-only, but the Mac can use suggestions created on the iPhone. Journal "is still most capable on the iPhone". — [9to5Mac, 2025-09-27](https://9to5mac.com/2025/09/27/macos-26-unlocks-the-real-potential-of-apples-journal-app/)
- **Criticism of the Mac/iPad UI:** it feels like a stretched iPhone app that ignores each platform's conventions, "functional but uninspiring" (search snippet). — [Numeric Citizen: Reviewing Apple Journal](https://numericcitizen.me/reviewing-apple-journal-a-summer-experiment/)

### Inferences
- The "moment" bundle (title, time range, photos, place or route, music, activity) is a strong model for grouping a Life day page by **episode** rather than by data source. For example: "Morning at the park, 09:10–10:30" shows the route, three photos, the music played and the steps together, instead of separate Photos, Location and Activity panels.
- Apple's Insights calendar uses a single dot per journaled day, which shows presence but not intensity. Life's year heatmap with selectable metrics goes further. Use a dot or tint for "has a note" and keep colour intensity for quantities.
- "Save without writing" corresponds to letting Life store a day as-is and treat notes as optional.
- Reviewers' complaints about a stretched iPhone layout are a warning for Life's desktop design: use the width (sidebar plus multi-column day page) rather than one centred mobile column.

### Gaps
- I could not read Apple's developer documentation for the JournalingSuggestions framework (the page body did not load), so I can't give the official list of suggestion item types.
- I found no source on what changed in Journal with the 2026 OS cycle (iOS 27 era). As of November 2026 the latest documented state here is the 26 release.
- I found no description of how Journal's map view draws pins or clusters, or of how its iOS 26 calendar or Insights screens look on Mac.

## 3. Apple Photos (iOS 18 redesign → iOS 26) and Google Photos: Memories, On This Day, Days/Months/Years, map, trips

### Takeaway
Apple's iOS 18 Photos (Sept 2024) merged the tabs into one scrolling page: a grid on top and auto-generated "Collections" (Recent Days, Trips, Memories, People & Pets, Pinned) below. Power users hated it for losing hierarchy and a "sense of place", for its suggestions-first launch view and for the removed Days view. Some reviewers, notably MacStories and Six Colors, came to like Recent Days, Trips and customization. Apple walked parts of it back: the Carousel was removed in beta and pinch-to-zoom returned. iOS 26 (Sept 2025) restored a Library/Collections tab bar. In Years, Months and All, Years shows a photo **from today's date in each year**. Google Photos took a different route: a Memories carousel at the top of the grid since 2020, and an auto-organised feed that was renamed "Moments" and moved into Collections in 2024–25.

### Cited Findings — Apple Photos iOS 18 (2024; older than current)
- **Layout:** "everything has been collapsed into a single page that you can scroll". The grid takes about 70% of the iPhone screen, and swiping down expands it, with a Years/Months control. Months uses "a dynamic grid of covers of different sizes". — [MacStories iOS 18 review, Photos](https://www.macstories.net/stories/ios-and-ipados-18-the-macstories-review/9)
- **Recent Days:** groups photos by day and works as "a visual calendar". An algorithm picks a key photo per day, and screenshots and receipts are hidden. Opening a day offers a movie, all items, a **map** of the day, or share, and the day can be sent to Journal. — [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/stories/ios-and-ipados-18-the-macstories-review/9)
- **Trips:** detected automatically and listed by date. Preview cards animate as you scroll, like "animated Polaroids of your life". — [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/stories/ios-and-ipados-18-the-macstories-review/9). Trips apply "dates and geotagging information in a smart way". Every Collection inherits the Memories idea of "a slideshow movie and a curated collection of items". — [Six Colors (Jason Snell)](https://sixcolors.com/post/2024/09/in-ios-18-photos-brings-collections-to-the-fore/)
- **Customization:** a "Customize & Reorder" control lets each section be reordered, hidden or pinned. Pinned Collections is "a user-customizable space for shortcuts". — [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/stories/ios-and-ipados-18-the-macstories-review/9)
- **Praise:** Collections are "a boon, a way in to your library" and "major improvements over long scrolls". Recent Days is "remarkably utilitarian". **Criticism from the same author:** the launch view "adds clutter", and Apple "should probably let people" opt out of it. — [Six Colors](https://sixcolors.com/post/2024/09/in-ios-18-photos-brings-collections-to-the-fore/)
- **Criticism roundup (June–Dec 2024):** Benjamin Mayo: "No tab bar means there's nothing to permanently ground navigation." Frank Rausch: "You don't get to form a fitting mental model / cognitive map". Mario Guzmán: "'Suggestions-first' rather than let me just go to where I need to go". Machiel: "There is no sense of place." Tuomas Hämäläinen wants "all the items in a regular grid so I can scan visually". Jason Snell called launching "into the MIDDLE of a scrollable area" "bananas". Michael Tsai: "I never want to see horizontal scrolling." Viticci later said "the modular approach … really works for me", and Apple's Billy Sorrentino said "we wanted to remove friction." — [Michael Tsai roundup](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2024/06/13/redesigned-photos-app-in-ios-18)
- **Recurring complaints and Apple's response (Nov 2024):** clutter; "redundant auto-generated albums and collections"; photos "no longer defaulting to full-screen viewing mode"; Library and Albums tabs removed; "Overemphasis on AI features at the expense of simple organization". In a MacRumors poll, 21.4% chose "I like it!" and 28.3% "It's okay". Apple removed the Carousel (daily highlights), reintroduced pinch-to-zoom and promised to remove borders around video. — [MacRumors, 2024-11-21](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/11/21/apples-photos-app-overhaul-controversial/)
- **Beta 5 changes (Aug 2024):** the Carousel was removed, "All Photos" shows more of the grid without a swipe, and Recently Saved was merged into Recent Days. — [9to5Mac, 2024-08-05](https://9to5mac.com/2024/08/05/ios-18-photos-app-changes/)
- **Days view removed in iOS 18:** Recent Days replaced it. One user missed the Days view's "mosaic style grid with varying sizes" (search snippet). — [MacRumors iOS 18 Photos guide](https://macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-photos); [MacRumors forum](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/ios-18-photos-app-all-the-new-features-and-design-updates.2433290)

### Cited Findings — Apple Photos iOS 26 / macOS 26 (current)
- **Tabs restored:** iOS 26 brings the tab bar back with Library and Collections, and Recent Days lives in Collections (search snippet). — [Tom's Guide: iOS 26 Photos overhaul](https://tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/ios-26-has-brought-yet-another-photos-app-overhaul-for-your-iphone-heres-how-its-different)
- **Years view = on-this-day per year:** Years "shows you a preview image from this date in that year". Months shows "a sampler of key moments (by Apple's algorithm) from each month". All is the continuous roll. The grid has five pinch-zoom levels: **Years, Months, 5 Across, 3 Across, Full image**. The menu appears when you scroll, and an "Aspect Ratio Grid" option shows uncropped thumbnails instead of squares, which the author recommends. — [5 Minute Photos: Library view](https://5minutephotos.substack.com/p/apple-photos-on-the-iphone-part-1)
- **Mac (current help page):** Years: "Quickly go to a specific year". Months: "View photos grouped by month, and organized by the days or the location they were taken". All Photos: a chronological grid. "On a trackpad, you can pinch open or closed on the month or year to change views", and double-click opens a month or year. — [Apple Support: Find photos and videos by date on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/photos/pht56eafa987)
- **Anecdotal, conflicting report:** one user says pinch-to-zoom-out into Years and Months stopped working after iOS 26.2.1, and another suggests swiping down instead. — [Apple Community thread](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256234546?page=2)

### Cited Findings — Google Photos
- **2020 redesign (older):** a "larger Memories carousel" at the top of the Photos tab, with new types "trips" and "the highlights from last week". Users can hide people or time periods and choose memory types. The grid has "larger thumbnails, auto-playing videos, and less white space between photos". The Search tab has an interactive map where you "pinch and zoom around the globe" to see "where you've taken the most photos around your hometown". Over 120M people viewed Memories monthly. — [Google blog, 2020-06-25](https://blog.google/products/photos/redesigned-google-photos/)
- **Nov 2024 (iOS) and Mar 2025 (Android):** the Memories tab left the bottom bar. The auto-organised feed is now "Moments" inside Collections, next to People & pets, Albums, Documents and Places, and its tile cycles through recent images. "Memories" now refers only to the carousel above the grid, and the bottom bar is Photos, Collections and Search/Ask. Top reader comment: "I think 'Memories' sounds way better than 'Moments'". — [9to5Google, 2024-11-28](https://9to5google.com/2024/11/28/google-photos-memories-moments); [9to5Google, 2025-03-20](https://9to5google.com/2025/03/20/google-photos-bottom-bar-android/)
- **Carousel card redesign (2025):** on-this-day and category memories get "abstract cut-out shapes" with background colours that vary per card, "a lot harder to miss as you scroll" (search snippet; page returned 403). — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-photos-memories-carousel-redesign-3636414)
- **Conflicting navigation history:** a TechRadar item in the search results said Memories was *moving to* the bottom navigation bar in the US. 9to5Google later reported it being removed from the bar (search snippet). Read these as successive changes, not current state.

### Inferences
- **Keep a stable, hierarchical frame.** The iOS 18 backlash was mostly about losing a "sense of place" and the suggestions-first launch view. Life's Day / Month / Year / On-this-day should be explicit, persistent tabs or a segmented control with URL-addressable state, with curated surfaces as opt-in sections.
- **Semantic zoom is an established pattern:** Years → Months → Days/grid via pinch or Ctrl-scroll (Apple's five levels; Mac trackpad pinch on a month or year). Life's year heatmap → month grid → day page is the same ladder, and Ctrl+wheel or a pinch to move between levels would feel native.
- **Apple's iOS 26 Years view doubles as "on this day"** by using today's date as the cover for every year. Life's On-this-day view could be a vertical list of year rows, each with a cover from that date, which matches what Apple ships.
- **Curate days:** hide screenshots, receipts and documents when picking a day cover or the day's highlights (Apple Recent Days). Immich exposes asset types and smart-search, so this filter can run in Life.
- **Give trips their own surface** with a cover, date range and place title, and render them as cards. Neither Apple nor Google draws trips as spans on a calendar (see Gaps).
- Offer an "aspect-ratio grid" toggle (uncropped) next to square crops. Users and reviewers favour it for scanning.

### Gaps
- I found no source showing how Apple titles Trips cards (place name plus date range?) or Recent Days cards (date only, or date plus place?). The screenshots were not accessible.
- I found no current (2026) description of Google Photos' timeline scrubber with year labels or of its map/Places heatmap. The only map description is from 2020.
- I found nothing on Photos or Journal changes in the 2026 OS cycle.
- No WWDC session transcript on the Photos redesign was retrieved. Apple's design rationale comes only through the quotes in Tsai's roundup.

## 4. Immich (immich.app): timeline scrubber, memories ("X years ago"), map, and UX issues from GitHub

### Takeaway
Immich copies the Google Photos model: a day-grouped timeline with a year-labelled scrubber rail, a "memories" strip on top of the timeline (same date in past years, refreshed daily) and a clustered OpenStreetMap map that you filter by date, album or person. Its GitHub tracker shows recurring friction: an auto-hiding scrubber that is hard to grab, missing or removed sticky date headers, memory ordering (newest-first vs. oldest-first), a single toggle for both the memories bar and the menu entry, and timeline performance on very large months.

### Cited Findings
- **Memories history:** mobile Memories shipped in v1.61.0 ("x years ago", up to 30 years back). The web version came in v1.63.0 (June 2023) and the mobile "On this day…" view in v1.66.0 (July 2023) (search snippets). — [newreleases: v1.61.0](https://newreleases.io/project/github/immich-app/immich/release/v1.61.0); [Immich roadmap](https://immich.app/roadmap/)
- **The web memory viewer has keyboard support and autoplay:** "feat(web): Add keyboard event support to memory view" (#2890) and "fix(web): memory pause autoplay when scrolling down" (#2923). — [Immich v1.63.0 release](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v1.63.0)
- **v1.129.0:** you can browse memories from yesterday, last week or tomorrow, and memory videos play on the web (search snippet). — [newreleases: v1.129.0](https://newreleases.io/project/github/immich-app/immich/release/v1.129.0)
- **Current behaviour (2026 write-up):** "Open Immich on any day and you will see a Memories section on your home timeline". "The selection refreshes daily". Memories use the taken date, not the upload date, so wrongly dated imports land on the wrong day. — [PixelUnion: On This Day in Immich (2026-05)](https://pixelunion.eu/blog/2026/05/immich-memories/)
- **Ordering debate:** issue #8275 (v1.99, Mar 2024): memories were shown "from newest to oldest, like timeline". The reporter wanted them "as events happened" (oldest → newest). Closed as completed via PR #8309. — [GitHub #8275](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/issues/8275)
- **One toggle for two surfaces:** turning off Memories hides both the bar at the top of the timeline and the Memories menu entry. A user asks to split them. — [GitHub discussion #32769](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/32769)
- **Memories not generated:** a report from v1.135.3 found no memories despite a library spanning many years (search snippet). — [GitHub #19728](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/issues/19728)
- **Auto-hiding scrubber (Aug 2026):** an elderly user did not notice the scroll thumb and was "too slow to drag it before it vanished again". The request is a setting to keep it permanently visible on mobile. — [GitHub discussion #30961](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/30961)
- **Snapping vs smooth scrubbing:** on mobile the scrubber is smooth on timelines under 12 months and otherwise snaps to day or month sections. A request asks for smooth scrubbing everywhere (search snippet). — [GitHub discussion #32551](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/32551)
- **Year rail and scale (v3.1.0, 2026):** the web "year rail shows the full date range", but dragging stalls at an arbitrary year on a library of about 2M assets. The reporter blames synchronous geometry pre-computation (a single 17.9 s long task on one big bucket) and cites a known limit of about 10k assets per month bucket (#21284). — [GitHub discussion #31360](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/31360)
- **Year labels request:** "Nice to have year labels like in Google Photos. It's helpful for large libraries to navigate" (search snippet). — [GitHub discussion #4902](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/4902)
- **Sticky date header:** requested since 2022 (keep the current Year/Month/Day pinned while scrolling), with web PRs #7824 (2024) and #25887 (2026). A 2026 commenter says the feature "seems removed in a later version". — [GitHub discussion #1667](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/1667)
- **Map:** a clustered map arrived in v1.55.0 and on the web in v1.56.0 (May 2023). Clusters are grouped by density and split as you zoom, down to single markers. "Click a cluster, and it expands into a timeline view of photos from that location". "You can filter the map by date range, album, or person". It uses OpenStreetMap, and photos without GPS don't appear. — [PixelUnion: Immich map view (2026-05)](https://pixelunion.eu/blog/2026/05/immich-map-view/); [Immich v1.55.0 release](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v1.55.0)

### Inferences
- Because Life gets its photos live from Immich, the Immich memories endpoint (same date, past years) is a natural feed for the On-this-day view. The #8275 debate suggests making year order a visible toggle, or defaulting to "most recent year first" with clear "N years ago" labels.
- A desktop Life panel avoids the mobile scrubber problems by using a persistent, always-visible year/month rail (like the year heatmap) instead of an auto-hiding thumb, plus a sticky date header in long day or month lists.
- Map clusters that expand into a chronological thumbnail strip, with date-range filtering, suit Life's day page: a map of the day's route with photo clusters that open the matching photos.
- Show a "no GPS" counter so photos without location don't vanish silently from map-based views.
- Performance warning: don't load whole months of Immich assets at once. Paginate by day and use thumbnails.

### Gaps

- I couldn't confirm whether PRs #7824 and #25887 (sticky date) were merged.

## 5. Year-at-a-glance heatmaps: Daylio "Year in Pixels", the GitHub contribution graph, readability and selectable metrics

### Takeaway
Two traditions exist. **Year in Pixels** (Daylio, bullet journals) uses a 12-month × 31-day grid with *categorical* colours (one per mood level) and leaves unlogged days blank. The **GitHub graph** uses 53 weeks × 7 weekdays with a *sequential* single-hue ramp whose thresholds are relative, per user, using quartiles after outlier removal. Relative scaling is easy to read but confuses users when one outlier day lightens everything else, and the dark theme's perceived inversion confuses some. Daylio lets you change what the pixels show (it added an activity picker), which matches Life's "selectable metric" idea.

### Cited Findings
- **Daylio:** "See the full year in one chart. Every dot is a day in your life. Export it as a picture and share it with friends!" Entries can be viewed "in list or calendar view". Users can "set your color theme". — [Daylio home](https://daylio.net/)
- **Daylio Year in Pixels is a Premium feature, and an "activity picker" was added** so you can view activities in Year in Pixels, not only mood (search snippet). — [Daylio on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/daylio-journal-mood-tracker/id1194023242)
- **Daylio stats colours** use "a color code that matches your mood colors", reused across frequency charts. Stats intervals are Last 30 Days, Last Year and All-Time. — [Daylio FAQ: Activity and Mood Statistics](https://daylio.net/faq/?p=5703)
- **Origin and layout of Year in Pixels:** created by Camille (@passioncarnets) in Dec 2015, inspired by Tim Urban's "Your Life in Weeks". The classic layout is 12 columns (months) × 31 rows (days). The advice is five *distinct hues* rather than shades of one colour, warm for high days and cool for low ones, avoiding red-green pairs (about 1 in 12 men have a colour vision deficiency). Leave missed days blank, because "backfilled moods are unreliable". — [Habitbox: Year in Pixels](https://habitbox.app/blog/year-in-pixels)
- **GitHub thresholds:** cutoffs are "calculated separately for each user" over the last 365 days ("We use quartiles after removing outliers"). Colours are "relative to what's there" in the displayed days, so a new busiest day lightens others, and GitHub won't publish more because it "would allow certain users to game them". User complaints: many commits shown as light green; earlier days looking "less intense" after two very busy days; the scale feeling inverted in dark theme. — [GitHub Community discussion #23261](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/23261)
- **Zero days excluded from the quartiles**, so the lowest non-zero quartile gets the lightest green (third-party explanation; search snippet). — [Gitblend: Understanding GitHub contribution graphs](https://gitblend.pages.dev/kb/understanding-github-contribution-graphs)
- **GitHub interaction:** "To view contributions made on a specific day, click the square corresponding to that day". Shift-click selects a range, capped at one month. A year list on the right switches years. The heading reads "NUMBER contributions in the last year". — [GitHub Docs: Viewing contributions on your profile](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/setting-up-and-managing-your-github-profile/managing-contribution-settings-on-your-profile/viewing-contributions-on-your-profile)
- **Apple Journal's iOS 18 Insights calendar** is a minimal variant: one purple dot for journaled days, with no intensity. — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/heres-everything-new-in-the-journal-app-for-ios-18/)

### Inferences
- **Pick the scale by metric type.** Quantities (spending, steps, photos taken, km travelled) use a sequential single-hue ramp of 4–5 bins plus a distinct "zero" colour and a "no data" state (hatched or outline). Categorical metrics (mood, "where": home, travel, office) use distinct hues, Year-in-Pixels style. Diverging metrics (spending vs. budget) use a two-hue ramp centred on the target.
- **Relative vs. absolute bins:** GitHub's relative quartiles are readable but confusing (outliers lighten everything). Life is a single-user tool, so show the bin edges in the legend ("0 · 1–3 · 4–9 · 10–24 · 25+ photos"), say whether the bins are quantiles or fixed, and consider clipping outliers into a top "25+" bin instead of letting one day rescale the year.
- **Separate "no data" from "zero":** Year in Pixels leaves unlogged days blank. For Life this matters for spending: no bank data is not the same as 0 spent.
- **Layout choice:** GitHub's weeks × weekdays exposes weekly rhythm (weekends), while months × days (Year in Pixels) makes "which date" and month comparison easy. A metric switcher above the heatmap plus a legend showing the bins and the current metric's unit match Daylio's activity picker.
- Let a click on a day open its page and Shift-click select a range, as GitHub does. Life could use the range for the trip or spending total.
- Choose hues that avoid red-green pairs, and test light and dark themes so dark doesn't read as "more".

### Gaps
- Daylio's exact Year in Pixels layout (grid orientation, how empty days look, tap behaviour) could not be confirmed from official pages. The help page fetched doesn't describe it, and screenshots weren't readable.
- GitHub's official docs don't state the colour algorithm. The quartile explanation comes from a community answer (the author's staff status is unconfirmed on the page) plus a third-party guide, and the two disagree slightly on the window (displayed days vs. last 365 days).
- No empirical readability research on calendar heatmaps was retrieved.

## 6. Concrete cross-cutting patterns: calendar cells with cover photos, trip spans, heatmap legends, on-this-day presentation, photo grids and lightboxes, empty days

### Takeaway
Most of these patterns have direct precedents: a cover photo per calendar day (Day One, Journey, Apple Recent Days), on-this-day as a per-year stack with "N years ago" (Day One, Immich, Apple's iOS 26 Years view, Google's carousel), and neutral empty days (Day One, Year in Pixels). **Trips drawn as spans across calendar days** have *no* precedent in the journaling and photo apps researched. Apple and Google present trips as separate cards or collections, so Life's spans are a novel pattern and need their own design care.

### Cited Findings
- **Calendar cells with a cover photo:** Day One (opt-in, user-chosen Favorite, otherwise the journal colour) — [Day One calendar](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/calendar-view-in-day-one/). Journey ("a color or a photo/video thumbnail") — [Journey calendar](https://support.journey.cloud/en/categories/app-interface-functionalities/articles/calendar-interface-in-journey). Apple Recent Days (algorithmic key photo per day, screenshots and receipts hidden) — [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/stories/ios-and-ipados-18-the-macstories-review/9).
- **Variable-size covers:** Apple's Months view uses "a dynamic grid of covers of different sizes" — [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/stories/ios-and-ipados-18-the-macstories-review/9). Users missed the old Days view's "mosaic style grid with varying sizes" (search snippet) — [MacRumors forum](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/ios-18-photos-app-all-the-new-features-and-design-updates.2433290).
- **Trips:** Apple detects trips and lists them by date as animated cards — [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/stories/ios-and-ipados-18-the-macstories-review/9). Google added "trips" as a Memories type — [Google blog 2020](https://blog.google/products/photos/redesigned-google-photos/).
- **On-this-day presentation:** Day One uses a Today tab with past years in "reverse-chronological order" — [Day One web release](https://dayoneapp.com/releases/on-this-day-now-available-on-web/). Immich puts a Memories strip on top of the timeline that refreshes daily — [PixelUnion](https://pixelunion.eu/blog/2026/05/immich-memories/). Apple iOS 26 Years shows "a preview image from this date in that year" — [5 Minute Photos](https://5minutephotos.substack.com/p/apple-photos-on-the-iphone-part-1). Google has a carousel above the grid — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2024/11/28/google-photos-memories-moments).
- **Controls over resurfacing:** Google lets users hide people or time periods and choose memory types — [Google blog 2020](https://blog.google/products/photos/redesigned-google-photos/). Immich users want the memories bar and menu entry toggled separately — [#32769](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/32769).
- **Photo grids and lightbox:** Google uses "larger thumbnails, auto-playing videos, and less white space" — [Google blog 2020](https://blog.google/products/photos/redesigned-google-photos/). Apple offers an Aspect Ratio Grid (uncropped) option — [5 Minute Photos](https://5minutephotos.substack.com/p/apple-photos-on-the-iphone-part-1). In Journal, tapping an item opens it full-screen — [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/reviews/apples-journal-app-journaling-for-all/). A complaint about iOS 18 was that photos stopped "defaulting to full-screen viewing mode" — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/11/21/apples-photos-app-overhaul-controversial/). The Immich memory viewer has keyboard support — [v1.63.0](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v1.63.0).
- **Empty days:** Day One makes them "white or black" by theme — [Day One calendar](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/calendar-view-in-day-one/). Year in Pixels leaves them blank and advises against backfilling — [Habitbox](https://habitbox.app/blog/year-in-pixels). Journey colours only days with at least one entry — [Journey calendar](https://support.journey.cloud/en/categories/app-interface-functionalities/articles/calendar-interface-in-journey).
- **Grounding navigation:** "No tab bar means there's nothing to permanently ground navigation" (Mayo), and a launch view that should be opt-out (Snell) — [Tsai roundup](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2024/06/13/redesigned-photos-app-in-ios-18); [Six Colors](https://sixcolors.com/post/2024/09/in-ios-18-photos-brings-collections-to-the-fore/).

### Inferences (design guidance for the Life panel; not sourced claims)
- **Month grid:** use a full-bleed cover crop per day, with the date number in a corner over a scrim, and add a tiny secondary marker row such as a note dot, a spending amount, or "+N photos". Pinned covers override automatic ones (Day One Favorite). Empty days show a plain neutral cell with no placeholder art.
- **Trip spans (novel):** borrow from calendar software rather than photo apps. Draw a rounded bar at the top of each week row, continuing across rows with a cut edge or arrow on the continued side, and label it only on the first segment of each row ("Lisbon · 12–18 Mar"). Clicking it opens a trip card with a cover, route and photo count, like Apple's Trips. Keep bars thin enough that covers stay visible.
- **Year heatmap:** add a metric switcher (photos, spending, km, notes, mood) and a legend with explicit bins plus a "no data" swatch. Draw trips on the heatmap as an overlay band or outlined runs of cells, not as colour, so the metric colour stays meaningful.
- **On this day:** use one row per past year ("1 year ago · 2025", Mon 6 Nov), each with a cover, one-line summary (place, spending, notes) and a photo strip. Offer newest-first/oldest-first and a "± few days" widening (Day One forum asks; Immich v1.129 yesterday/last-week browsing). Hide years without data, or collapse them into one line ("2019, 2021: nothing recorded").
- **Lightbox:** open full-screen by default, with keyboard arrows, Esc and a filmstrip. Keep day context (date, place) in the lightbox header.
- **Semantic zoom:** Year → Month → Day via Ctrl+wheel or pinch on a trackpad, mirroring Apple's Years/Months/All ladder, while still keeping explicit tabs so navigation stays grounded.

### Gaps
- I found **no** journaling or photo app that draws multi-day trips as spans over a month-grid calendar. The trip-span guidance above is inferred from general calendar software conventions and is not sourced from the apps researched.
- I didn't verify app-store or Reddit review sentiment directly. Opinions here come from tech-press reviews, MacRumors forums and GitHub; Reddit pages surfaced only through mirror domains, which I didn't use.
- I could rarely confirm screenshot contents. Most help pages had images without alt text, and several press pages (AppleInsider, Neowin, Android Authority) returned HTTP 403.
